booduhclips

A desktop app for making and posting short clips.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

booduhclips runs on a single Windows PC. It takes a long video, cuts it into short vertical clips, writes a caption for each one, and — once the owner reviews and approves a clip — uploads it to that person's own social accounts.

What it does

  • Downloads or opens a source video the owner supplies.
  • Transcribes it and picks out the strongest moments.
  • Renders vertical clips and drafts a caption for each platform.
  • Uploads an approved clip to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube or X account.
  • Reads back the view and like counts of those posts so the owner can see how each clip did.

Who it is for

One person: the owner of the computer it is installed on, posting to their own accounts. There are no other users, no accounts to sign up for, and no shared service.
