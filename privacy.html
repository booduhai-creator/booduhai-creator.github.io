Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

HomeTerms of Service

The short version. booduhclips runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers and no database. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no "us" to send it to. The only data that leaves your computer is the clip you choose to post, which goes directly to the platform you chose.

1. What the app stores, and where

Everything the app knows is kept in files on your own computer, inside the app's folder:

  • Your videos and clips — the source videos you gave it and the clips it rendered.
  • Captions and notes — the text drafted for each clip, plus campaign details you typed in.
  • Access tokens — the keys TikTok, YouTube and X issue when you connect an account, so the app can post on your behalf without storing your password. The app never sees or stores your social media passwords.
  • Post statistics — view, like and comment counts read back from your own posts.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app's folder deletes all of it.

2. What the app reads from TikTok, and why

  • Your basic profile (user.info.basic) — your handle and avatar, shown in the app so you can confirm the right account is connected.
  • Your follower count (user.info.stats) — displayed on the app's Accounts screen.
  • Your own video list (video.list) — to find the link and view count of posts the app made, so you can see how each clip performed.
  • Posting (video.publish, video.upload) — to upload a clip you selected to your own account.

The app only ever reads the connected account's own data. It does not read other users' data, browse other people's content, or collect anything about anyone else.

3. What is never collected

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no browsing history. The app has no telemetry of any kind.

4. Sharing

Nothing is shared or sold, because nothing is collected. The only outbound data is the video and caption you chose to post, sent directly to the platform you chose to post it to. That platform's own privacy policy then applies to the post.

5. AI processing

Captions are drafted with an AI service. When that happens, the transcript text of your own clip is sent to that service to write the caption and is not used to train it. Your video file itself is never sent — transcription and rendering happen on your computer.

6. Your control

  • Disconnect any time — Accounts screen in the app, or revoke access in the platform's own settings. Either stops the app immediately.
  • Delete everything — delete the app's folder. There is no copy anywhere else and nothing to request from us.
  • Data requests — we hold no data about you, so there is nothing to export or erase on our side.

7. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Updates to this policy will appear here with a new date at the top.
