1. What booduhclips is

booduhclips is a desktop application that runs on the user's own computer. It cuts long videos into short clips, drafts captions, and — at the user's instruction — uploads those clips to social media accounts the user owns and has connected. It is used by one person on their own accounts. There is no hosted service, no sign-up, and no account with us.
